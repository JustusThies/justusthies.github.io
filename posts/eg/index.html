EG2026 - Program Chair

2026, May 19    
Belen Masia  Justus Thies 
EG

Eurographics 2026, the premier annual conference of the European Association for Computer Graphics, will take place May 4–8, 2026 in Aachen, Germany, bringing together leading researchers, industry innovators, and practitioners for a week of cutting‑edge advances in computer graphics. Hosted at the Cineplex Kinopark Aachen, the conference features a rich technical program, public events, and a dedicated social gathering at Aachen’s historic Coronation Hall. This year’s lineup of keynote speakers includes George Drettakis, Jaakko Lehtinen, Lourdes Agapito, Bernd Bickel, Anatole Lécuyer, and Björn Ommer, reflecting the breadth and momentum of contemporary graphics research.
